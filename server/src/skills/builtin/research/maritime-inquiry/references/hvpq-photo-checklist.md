# HVPQ Photo Upload Verification Checklist

## Purpose
Pre-upload verification checklist for HVPQ photographs to ensure OCIMF SIRE 2.0 Code 11.1.19 compliance.

## 5-Step Verification Process

### Step 1: Viewpoint Correct (视角正确)
- Photo angle and perspective match the HVPQ photo requirement note
- Not from the wrong side (e.g., starboard photo must show starboard side)
- No mirror-image or reversed perspective

### Step 2: Representative of Actual Condition (代表实际状况)
- Photo taken during current visit or recent period
- Condition shown matches actual onboard status at time of upload
- No significant changes since photo taken
- Date stamp or upload date verifiable

### Step 3: Fairleads & Equipment Visible (导缆孔及设备可见)
- Fairleads clearly visible and identifiable
- Associated equipment (cleats, rollers, chocks) within frame
- No obstruction blocks view of critical equipment
- Equipment condition assessable from photo

### Step 4: Free of Clutter/Salvage (无杂物/临时搭材)
- No temporary repairs, improvised arrangements, or salvage gear
- No unrelated items (containers, cargo gear, hoses) obstructing view
- Normal operating condition, not a staged/cleaned-up scene
- No construction/maintenance in progress within frame

### Step 5: Matches HVPQ Photo Note (与HVPQ照片说明一致)
- Correct HVPQ photo number assigned (e.g., Photo No. 19 = Poop deck looking from midships to starboard)
- Content matches HVPQ photo requirement text description
- If replacing old photo, covers same area/equipment as original note
- No mismatch between photo number, content, and description

## Approval Workflow
1. Second Officer — Prepares & verifies (signs off steps 1-5)
2. Master — Reviews & approves
3. Superintendent — Shore-side verification (optional but recommended)

## Technical Requirements
- All photos must be original — no edits, cropping adjustments, or filters
- High resolution, well-lit, in focus
- Blurry or dark photos are not acceptable
- Applies to ALL HVPQ photo uploads, not just specific photo numbers
